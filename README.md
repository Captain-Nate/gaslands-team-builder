# Gaslands Team Builder

Unofficial team builder for the Gaslands third-edition playtest rules (Vehicles 5.3, Drivers 5.2, Sponsors 5.2).
Pick vehicles, weapons, upgrades, driver archetypes, perks and a sponsor; cans, build slots and team legality are checked live.
The page link carries the current team, so a bookmark or a texted link reopens the same build.

Live: https://captain-nate.github.io/gaslands-team-builder/

## Rules from your own rulebook

If you own the Gaslands rulebook, load your PDF chapters (Vehicles, Drivers, Sponsors) on the home page to unlock
tap-for-rules on every item. The PDFs are read in your browser and the text stays on your device; nothing is sent
anywhere, and no rule text ships with this site. Don't have it yet? Get it from Planet Smasher Games.

Rule summaries are short paraphrases for quick reference; the rulebook chapters are the source of truth.
